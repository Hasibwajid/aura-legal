# AURA Child Safety Standards

_Last updated: 2026-09-02_

AURA maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

## Prohibited content and behavior

AURA prohibits:

- child sexual abuse material,
- sexual exploitation or sexualization of minors,
- grooming or soliciting minors for sexual purposes,
- sextortion involving minors,
- child trafficking or exploitation,
- sharing links to prohibited material,
- attempts to create, distribute, obtain, or promote CSAM.

These standards apply to photos, profile information, Aura posts, usernames, and all other activity on AURA.

## Reporting concerns in AURA

Users can report an Aura or account from the relevant user's Aura screen:

1. Open the user's Aura.
2. Open the available actions (⋯ menu in the top bar).
3. Select **Report**.
4. Confirm the report.

Users may also block another account from the same menu, immediately hiding their content and preventing further contact.

Users may also report urgent child-safety concerns by emailing:

**lead2ai.ops@gmail.com**

Include the AURA username and relevant details. Do not email, download, copy, or redistribute suspected CSAM.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate law-enforcement authority.

## Our response

AURA reviews child-safety reports and may:

- remove violating content,
- suspend or permanently terminate accounts,
- prevent contact between affected accounts,
- preserve relevant records where legally required,
- report confirmed or suspected violations to the appropriate regional or national authorities.

Where required by applicable law, AURA reports apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant local authority.

## Compliance

AURA complies with applicable child-safety laws and cooperates with lawful requests from authorized authorities.
